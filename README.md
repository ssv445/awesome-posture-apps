# Awesome Posture Apps [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> Apps that nudge you to sit better while you work at a computer, grouped by how each one notices your posture.

Every fact here comes from the maker's own website or App Store listing, checked on 21 October to 8 November 2026. Prices and features change, so check the maker's page before you buy.

**Disclosure:** I'm Shyam Verma, and I make Poxture App, one of the apps on this list. Every app gets the same kind of line here, with no ranking.

## Webcam

- [AlignKit](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/purepose-ai-posture-monitor/id6764853072?mt=12) - Uses your camera or AirPods, and alerts you with a sound, a screen blur or another style. Mac (macOS 13 or later). Subscription.
- [ApexForm](https://apexform.me) - Uses your webcam, or AirPods with the camera off, and nudges you with a soft sound, a card that says what slipped, or both. Mac (macOS 14 or later), in public beta. Free plan; Pro is a subscription.
- [BaroSit](https://barosit.com) - Uses your webcam and sends a desktop notification when one posture lasts too long, or a glow, pop-up or sound you pick. Mac (macOS 12 or later), Windows, web. Free in beta; a paid Pro plan is planned.
- [BetterPose](https://betterpose.co/) - Uses your webcam and shows an on-screen notification and a live 0 to 100 score. Mac, Windows. Subscription.
- [BetterPosture](https://betterposture.app) - Checks with your webcam every five minutes by default, and shows one small overlay when your head drifts forward. Apple Silicon Mac (macOS 13 or later). Free; the Pro dashboard is paid once.
- BLiiNK - Uses your webcam to check your posture and count your blinks, and shows a notification that goes away once you fix it. Windows 10 or 11, Macs with M1 or M2 chips. Subscription. Its website no longer loads; these facts are from copies of its pages from August 2025.
- [Dorso](https://dorso.app/) - Uses your Mac's camera or AirPods, and blurs, tints or borders your screen until you sit up. Mac (macOS 13 or later). Free from GitHub, or paid once on the Mac App Store. Open source ([GitHub](https://github.com/tldev/dorso)).
- [Nekoze](https://nekoze.app) - Uses your camera and tells you when you are hunched over. Mac (macOS 12 or later). Free.
- [Plumb](https://plumbcoach.com/) - Uses your Mac's camera, waits to see whether a slouch lasts, then shows a soft glow at the screen edge, with or without sound. Macs with Apple silicon. Lifetime paid once, or a subscription.
- [Posture Monitor](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6751619063) - Uses your Mac's built-in camera or a USB camera, and sends a notification with a menu-bar indicator. Mac. Free; a Pro unlock is paid once.
- [Posture Reminder AI](https://posturereminderapp.com) - Uses your Mac's camera, an external camera or your iPhone, and sends a macOS notification. Mac (macOS 26 or later). Subscription.
- [PostureKind](https://posturekind.com) - Uses your webcam, or timed reminders with the camera off, and shows an on-screen cue with an optional quiet sound. Web, Chrome, Mac; you sign in. Subscription, after a 7-day trial.
- [Posturion](https://posturion.app) - Uses your built-in or external webcam, and plays one of 15 sounds or sends a desktop notification. Mac (macOS 14 or later). One free hour for new users, then a subscription.
- [Poxture App](https://poxture.com/) - Checks your posture through your Mac's camera for eight habits, and shows a soft glow at the top of the screen with a card that shows how to fix the habit. Apple Silicon Mac (macOS 15 or later). Free 1 hour a day; all day is paid once.
- [SitApp](https://sitapp.app) - Uses your webcam and on-device AI; when you slouch, a droid pokes its head into the corner of your screen. Mac (macOS 12 or later), Windows 10 or later, Linux. Free 1 hour a day; Pro is a subscription, Lifetime is paid once.
- [SitWit](https://sitwit.app) - Uses your webcam and sends a notification, with a colour in the menu bar. Mac. Free; Premium is a subscription.
- [Slouch Sniper](https://slouchsniper.com) - Uses your webcam and on-device AI, and dims your screen until you sit up. Windows, Apple Silicon Mac. Paid once.
- [Spina](https://spina.fit/) - Uses your webcam and shows a score, then after twenty seconds a small window with you and a posture outline. Mac (macOS 12 or later), in early access. Paid once.
- [Straighty](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/straighty/id6736836584) - Uses your Mac's built-in camera, and reminds you with a soft voice, a chime or a notification. Mac (macOS 15 or later). Free.
- [SuperShrimp](https://supershrimp.io/) - Uses your webcam and shows a popup where you see yourself, with feedback on how to correct, plus XP, levels and a leaderboard. Mac, Windows, Linux. Paid once.
- [TryWorkWell](https://tryworkwell.com) - Uses your webcam, or AirPods on a Mac, and sends one system notification per slouch, plus eye, water and focus timers. Mac (macOS 12 or later), Windows 10 or 11. Subscription, or Lifetime paid once.

## AirPods or headphones

- [Posture Pal](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/posture-pal-improve-alert/id1590316152) - Uses the motion sensors in AirPods (3rd generation), AirPods Pro, AirPods Max or Beats Fit Pro, and sends an alert that can also show on your Apple Watch. iPhone (iOS 17 or later), Mac (macOS 14 or later). Free; Pro and plans are in-app purchases.
- [SitTall](https://sittall.app/) - Uses the motion sensors in your AirPods or Beats Fit Pro, and sends a Mac notification when your head drifts forward, tilts to one side or slowly sinks. Apple Silicon Mac (macOS 14 or later). Paid once.

## Wearable

- Lumo Lift - No longer made. A sensor clipped below your collarbone that vibrated when you slouched, with an iPhone or Android app. Not sold since June 2018.
- [Upright GO](https://www.uprightpose.com) - A small sensor on your back that buzzes when you slouch, while sitting, walking or moving. Works with the free UPRIGHT app for iPhone and Android. The device is paid once.

## Break timer

- [LookAway](https://lookaway.com/) - Reminds you to take breaks on a timer, with posture and blink reminders you can turn on. Mac (macOS 13 or later). Paid once after a 7-day trial, or a subscription on the Mac App Store.
- [Stretchly](https://github.com/hovancik/stretchly) - Reminds you to take breaks: by default a 20-second mini break every 10 minutes and a 5-minute break every 30 minutes. Mac (macOS 13 or later), Windows, Linux. Free. Open source.
- [Time Out](https://www.dejal.com/timeout/) - Shows a break screen with a scene you pick, on a schedule you set. Mac (macOS 26 or later; an older version runs on macOS 10.15 or later). Free, with paid extras.

## Browser-based

- [SitCoach](https://www.sitcoach.com) - Checks for slouching and screen distance with your webcam in a browser tab; the tab icon changes colour, then a notification shows. Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a desktop. Free.
- [SitSense](https://sitsense.app) - A posture coach in a browser tab that uses your webcam; the reef dims and a companion comes to find you. Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge, and a Chrome extension. Free; Pro is a subscription.
- [StopSlouching](https://stopslouching.app) - Uses your webcam in a browser or the Windows app, and sends a notification with a 30-second stretch. Web, Windows 10 or later, a Chrome extension; a Mac app is "coming soon". Subscription, or paid once.
